Daniel Barnard

CEO & Founder

Daniel is the CEO of PocketVC, where he's rethinking how investors and startups connect. A venture capitalist by background, he's sourced and backed frontier-tech opportunities that are shaping tomorrow.

He continues to invest with Ultratech Capital Partners, while PocketVC is his way of fixing the broken match-making process between founders and VCs. Earlier in his career, Daniel trained at one of the world's largest international banks, gaining hands-on experience in global markets before transitioning into the venture space.

His mission: level the playing field for founders and give investors a smarter way to spot the next big thing.
