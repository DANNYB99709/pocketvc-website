Is PocketVC free to use?

PocketVC Team • 15 January 2025 • 2 min read

Yes! PocketVC is completely free for startups. We believe that great startups shouldn't be limited by their ability to pay for access to investors.

For Startups:

  • Free forever: No subscription fees, no per-introduction charges
  • Unlimited matches: Connect with as many relevant VCs as you want
  • Full platform access: All features and tools are included

For VCs:

  • Free during beta: No charges while we perfect the platform
  • Future pricing: We'll introduce reasonable pricing for VCs post-launch
  • Value-based: Pricing will be based on the value we provide to your deal flow

Our mission is to democratize access to venture capital, so we're committed to keeping the platform accessible to startups.
