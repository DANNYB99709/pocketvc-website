How do you protect my data and privacy?

PocketVC Team • 15 January 2025 • 3 min read

Data security and privacy are fundamental to everything we do at PocketVC. We understand that startups and VCs share sensitive information, and we protect it with enterprise-grade security.

Data Protection:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access controls: Strict access controls and authentication
  • Regular audits: Third-party security audits and penetration testing
  • GDPR compliant: Full compliance with European data protection regulations

Privacy Controls:

  • You control your data: You decide what information to share and with whom
  • No data selling: We never sell or share your data with third parties
  • Transparent matching: You can see why you were matched with someone
  • Easy deletion: You can delete your account and data at any time

Read our full Privacy Policy for complete details.
