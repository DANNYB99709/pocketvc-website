Who is PocketVC for?

Frequently Asked Question
General

PocketVC is designed for European deep tech startups seeking funding and investors looking for high-quality opportunities in the deep tech space.

For Startups:

  • European deep tech companies
  • Pre-seed to Series A stage
  • Companies with strong IP or technical differentiation
  • Teams with deep technical expertise

For Investors:

  • VCs focused on deep tech
  • Corporate venture arms
  • Angel investors with technical backgrounds
  • Family offices interested in deep tech
