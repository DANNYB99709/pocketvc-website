## What We Do
PocketVC is a UK-based platform that revolutionizes how venture capital firms discover and connect with innovative startups. We use advanced AI-powered matching technology to create meaningful, high-quality connections between VCs and startups, focusing on deep tech and frontier technology sectors.

## Our Mission
To democratize access to venture capital by providing free, intelligent matching between startups and the right investors, while helping VCs discover relevant, high-quality deal flow.

## Key Services
- **AI-Powered Matching:** Advanced algorithms that understand startup DNA and VC investment thesis
- **Curated Deal Flow:** Quality over quantity approach for VCs
- **Free Startup Access:** No cost for startups to join and get matched
- **Deep Tech Focus:** Specialized in frontier technology sectors
- **European Market:** Primary focus on UK and European markets

## Target Audience
- **Startups:** Early-stage to Series A companies seeking funding
- **VCs:** Venture capital firms looking for quality, relevant deal flow
- **Geographic:** UK and European markets initially

## What Makes Us Different
1. **Quality Over Quantity:** We focus on curated, relevant matches rather than mass outreach
2. **AI Beyond Keywords:** Our matching goes far beyond simple keyword matching
3. **Free for Startups:** We believe every innovative company deserves access to funding
4. **Deep Tech Specialization:** We understand the unique needs of frontier technology companies
5. **European Focus:** We're built for the European tech ecosystem
