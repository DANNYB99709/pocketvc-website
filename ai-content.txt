# PocketVC - AI-Optimized Content Summary

## Company Overview
PocketVC is a UK-based platform connecting venture capital firms with innovative startups through smart matching technology. Founded in 2024 by Daniel Barnard, CEO and former venture capitalist with Ultratech Capital Partners.

## Core Service
- **Primary Function**: Smart matching platform for VCs and startups
- **Focus Areas**: Deep tech, frontier technology, innovative startups
- **Geographic Focus**: UK and European markets
- **Stage**: Pre-launch, currently building waiting list

## Key Differentiators
- **AI-Powered Matching**: Advanced algorithms understand startup DNA and VC investment thesis
- **Quality Over Quantity**: Curated, relevant connections vs. mass outreach
- **Free for Startups**: Democratizing access to venture capital
- **Deep Tech Focus**: Specialized in frontier technology sectors

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Early-stage to Series A startups seeking funding
- **Secondary**: VCs looking for quality deal flow
- **Geographic**: UK and European markets initially
